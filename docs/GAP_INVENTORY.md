Initial audit baseline: `e3f5247fafc08e143562b6a0dacd4d397fe62641`, reviewed on 2026-10-29 against the public AG Grid 36.2.0 feature inventory and updated with the vertical-span and UI-localization checkpoints. ## Current and next checkpoint acceptance

The vertical-span checkpoint renders one owner cell across adjacent eligible rows, omits covered cells, and retains an offscreen owner when its tail intersects the viewport. It preserves pin regions and original logical ARIA positions, supports owner editing/undo and keyboard/Find navigation, bounds memory/comparison work, and clips at pages, groups, details, full-width rows and pin/depth boundaries. Unsupported two-dimensional combinations and rectangular operations are explicit rather than silently rendered incorrectly. Its guide and verification record own the precise limits and executed results.

The next React priorities are RTL/touch and structured translation codes for remaining diagnostics and the substantial filter/menu, formula-editing and remote-write workflows above. A translation seam without translated built-in workflows, pointer handlers without touch tests, or backend request types without an editable conflict/retry flow will remain partial. Combined spans require a rectangle-ownership design before enabling existing range or export operations over merged cells.

Complete unit, production build, real-browser and site checks must pass with one local worker, followed by exact-commit CI/Pages and live-flow verification. The [verification record](VERIFICATION.md) records executed results. No manual screen-reader, touch-device or performance certification is inferred from automated checks.
